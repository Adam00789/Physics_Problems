Theory Summary

General solution of mẍ + bẋ + kx = 0 with characteristic roots r = (−b ± √(b²−4mk)) / 2m:

CaseConditionForm of Solution
Underdamped b² < 4mk x(t) = e−γt(C₁cosωdt + C₂sinωdt),  ωd=√(ω₀²−γ²)
Critically Damped b² = 4mk x(t) = (C₁ + C₂t)e−γt
Overdamped b² > 4mk x(t) = C₁er₁t + C₂er₂t, both r negative
