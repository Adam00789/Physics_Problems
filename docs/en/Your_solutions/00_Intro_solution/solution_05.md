# Task 05 – Trigonometry and Vector Components

## Problem Statement

A vector $\vec{A}$ has magnitude $15$ and makes an angle $\theta = 60^\circ$ with the horizontal axis.

Determine its horizontal and vertical components.

---

## Theory

A vector in two dimensions can be decomposed into components using trigonometry:

$$
A_x = A \cos \theta
$$

$$
A_y = A \sin \theta
$$

where:

- $A_x$ is the horizontal component  
- $A_y$ is the vertical component  

---

## Step-by-Step Solution

Given:

$$
A = 15, \qquad \theta = 60^\circ
$$

---

### Horizontal component

$$
A_x = A \cos \theta
$$

$$
A_x = 15 \cos 60^\circ
$$

Using $\cos 60^\circ = \frac{1}{2}$:

$$
A_x = 15 \cdot \frac{1}{2}
$$

$$
A_x = 7.5
$$

---

### Vertical component

$$
A_y = A \sin \theta
$$

$$
A_y = 15 \sin 60^\circ
$$

Using $\sin 60^\circ = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$:

$$
A_y = 15 \cdot \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}
$$

$$
A_y = \frac{15\sqrt{3}}{2}
$$

---

## Final Result

$$
A_x = 7.5
$$

$$
A_y = \frac{15\sqrt{3}}{2}
$$

---

## Interpretation

- The horizontal component represents the projection of the vector onto the x-axis.  
- The vertical component represents the projection onto the y-axis.  
- Since $\theta = 60^\circ$, the vector has a larger vertical component than horizontal, indicating a steep upward direction.
