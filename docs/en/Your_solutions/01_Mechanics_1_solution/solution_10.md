# Task 10 – Kinematics

## Problem Statement

$$
\vec{r}(t) = (a\cos(\omega t), b\sin(\omega t), bt)
$$

---

## Theory

Helical motion.

---

## Step-by-Step Solution

Trajectory:

$$
\frac{x^2}{a^2} + \frac{y^2}{b^2} = 1, \quad z = bt
$$

Speed:

$$
|\vec{v}| = \sqrt{a^2\omega^2\sin^2 + b^2\omega^2\cos^2 + b^2}
$$

---

## Final Result

Helix trajectory.

---

## Interpretation

Special case: $a=b$ gives circular helix.
