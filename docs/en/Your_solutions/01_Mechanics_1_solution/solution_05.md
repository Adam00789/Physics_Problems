
# Task 05 – Relative Velocity

## Problem Statement

A river flows east at $2$ m/s. A boat moves at $5$ m/s relative to water and wants to go north.

Find heading angle and crossing time for width $200$ m.

---

## Theory

To cancel drift:

$$
v_{\text{boat},x} = -2
$$

---

## Step-by-Step Solution

Let $\theta$ be west of north:

$$
5\sin\theta = 2
$$

$$
\sin\theta = \frac{2}{5}
$$

$$
\theta \approx 23.6^\circ
$$

Vertical speed:

$$
v_y = 5\cos\theta = \sqrt{25 - 4} = \sqrt{21}
$$

Time:

$$
t = \frac{200}{\sqrt{21}} \approx 43.6 \text{ s}
$$

---

## Final Result

Angle: $23.6^\circ$ west of north  
Time: $\approx 43.6$ s

---

## Interpretation

Boat must aim upstream to counter current.
