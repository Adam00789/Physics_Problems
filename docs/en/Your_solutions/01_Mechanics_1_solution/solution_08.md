# Task 08 – Circular Motion

## Problem Statement

Find centripetal acceleration at Earth's equator.

---

## Theory

$$
a_c = \omega^2 R
$$

$$
\omega = \frac{2\pi}{T}
$$

---

## Step-by-Step Solution

$$
T = 86400 \text{ s}, \quad R = 6.378 \times 10^6
$$

$$
\omega \approx 7.27 \times 10^{-5}
$$

$$
a_c \approx 0.034 \text{ m/s}^2
$$

---

## Final Result

$$
a_c \approx 0.034 \text{ m/s}^2
$$

---

## Interpretation

Centripetal acceleration is small compared to gravity.
