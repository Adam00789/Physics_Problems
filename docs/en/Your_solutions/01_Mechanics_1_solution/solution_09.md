# Task 09 – Momentum Comparison

## Problem Statement

Compare momentum of:

- Fly: $2$ g at $10$ m/s  
- Ball: $60$ g at $1$ m/s

---

## Theory

$$
p = mv
$$

---

## Step-by-Step Solution

Convert:

$$
0.002 \cdot 10 = 0.02
$$

$$
0.06 \cdot 1 = 0.06
$$

---

## Final Result

Tennis ball has greater momentum.

---

## Interpretation

Mass dominates over velocity here.
